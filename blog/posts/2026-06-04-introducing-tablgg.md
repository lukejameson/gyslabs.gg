It's a Wednesday night, im looking for what restaurants have space for two on Friday night around 7pm. Easy problem, right?

It's probably a problem you have faced in the past when trying to eat out. You rack your brain for restaurants, Google around places you know to see if they have space, check two or three different places, then either book the same place as usual or give up because they don't have space. Cut to your takeaway app of choice with every quick and easy option at your fingertips and your chow mein being dropped off at your door in an hour.

Why can't something like food.gg exist for the wonderful restaurants on our island? Why can't you just open up a page, type in what and when you want and get a list of all the places with a table and book it quick and easy?

Well, now you can. Introducing tabl.gg, our latest application to make your life easier when trying to dine out. Simply load up tabl.gg, type in what you want "Chinese for 2 on Saturday at 7pm" and tabl.gg will do all the searching around for you, find the restaurants with space, find you a table at your chosen time and make it a two-click booking away.

No filling out your email, phone number and name every time. tabl.gg keeps all that information safe and secure so that instead of worrying about where you can eat, you can worry about what you will eat.

Currently not all restaurants are on tabl.gg. We are currently hosting places that use ResDiary as their booking system. We are hoping to be able to provide a lightweight booking system for restaurants that are looking for a fast and convenient booking platform in the near future. If you are interested in working with us then reach out at contact@gsylabs.gg or contact@tabl.gg.
